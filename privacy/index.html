Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

OTHERDESK is operated by Alan Satis, Portugal ("we"). Contact: contact@otherdesk.com

1. Information We Collect

When you use OTHERDESK, we collect information you provide directly, such as your email address when you create an account. We also collect usage data including check-in activity and favourite venues. The app additionally reads your device location while you are using it, as described in section 2.

2. Location

The OTHERDESK mobile app uses your precise device location. The first time the map screen finishes loading, your device asks you to allow location access. Access is requested for foreground use only — while the app is open on screen. We never collect your location in the background.

If you allow access, the app reads your position once and then re-reads it roughly every 5 seconds for as long as the map screen stays open. It reads your position once more when you open a venue page. Your location is used to centre the map on you, to select the nearest supported city on first launch, and to show how far each venue is from you.

Your location is held only in the app's memory on your device for as long as the screen is open. It is never written to our database, never attached to your account and never included in our analytics. The map itself is provided by Mapbox, whose software may collect anonymous usage and location telemetry; you can turn this off at any time from the (i) button on the map.

Allowing location is optional. If you decline, the app still works — you can browse every city and venue, check in, and save favourites. The map shows an "Enable location to see venues near you" prompt, distances are hidden, and nothing about your position is collected. You can change your decision at any time in your device's system settings.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and improve the OTHERDESK service, including displaying venue information, managing your account, and processing purchases.

4. Analytics

OTHERDESK uses PostHog Cloud EU for analytics. Analytics are consent-gated and default to opted-out. No session replay or autocapture is enabled. You can opt out of analytics at any time from the Profile screen in the app.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers who help us operate the app: Supabase (database and authentication), RevenueCat (purchases), PostHog (analytics, only with your consent), Google and Apple (sign-in, if you choose them) and Mapbox (maps).

6. Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active. Check-in data expires after 3 hours to ensure freshness. You can delete your account at any time from the Profile screen, or as described on the Delete account page.

7. Your Rights Under GDPR

OTHERDESK serves users in the European Union, and you hold the following rights over your personal data under the General Data Protection Regulation:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected.
  • Erasure — have your data deleted. You can do this yourself at any time from the Profile screen, which removes your account and its associated data.
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection and restriction — object to, or ask us to restrict, our processing of your data. Analytics are opt-in and can be turned off at any time from the Profile screen.
  • Withdraw consent — where processing relies on your consent, withdraw it at any time without affecting processing carried out before you withdrew it.

To exercise any of these rights, email contact@otherdesk.com. We respond within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority (in Portugal, the CNPD).

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at contact@otherdesk.com.
