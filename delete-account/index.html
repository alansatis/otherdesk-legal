Delete your OTHERDESK account

Version 1.0 · Effective 30 September 2026 · Version history

You can delete your account and its associated data at any time.

In the app

  1. Open OTHERDESK and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap "Delete account" and confirm with "Delete my account".

Without the app

Email contact@otherdesk.com from the address linked to your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your account, email address, favourites and check-ins are permanently deleted.

What may be kept

Purchase records held by Apple, Google and RevenueCat are kept by them as required by tax and accounting law. Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription — cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.
